## 🧠 About Me
I am a Master’s graduate in **AI and Automation** at University West (Högskolan Väst), with industrial research experience at **Volvo Cars**.

My work sits between research and deployment: evaluating **generative and agentic AI** in industrial workflows, building **computer vision** pipelines, and studying **robust control**. For my thesis I developed **V-HAARF** and a post-implementation evaluation method, and applied them to **CTPBot**, an agentic tool for test vehicle allocation.

I care about AI that is useful in real factories and labs — measurable, responsible, and possible to maintain.
